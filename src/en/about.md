This site is for people trying to understand what is happening to them, or to someone close, who would like to read about it in plain language rather than textbook language.

## What it is and what it is not

Here you will find information written to be useful and honest. But it is educational. It does not diagnose, it does not replace a psychologist, psychotherapist or doctor, and it does not promise that a particular method will help everyone. If something in the articles sounds like your experience, that is a reason to talk to a professional, not a conclusion.

## How the articles are made

Facts rest on sources listed at the end of each article. Every article shows when it was published and when it was last updated. When an article has been reviewed by a professional, that is stated. If it has not, there is a clear note. Topics that may be heavy begin with a warning, and you can decide whether to read on.

If you spot a mistake or something that could be said better, we would be glad to hear it.

## Privacy

There are no accounts, comments or forms here. Search runs in your browser and what you type is not sent anywhere.
