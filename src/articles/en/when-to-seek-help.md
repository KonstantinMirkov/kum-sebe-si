We often hear: “Other people have bigger problems.” True, but that is not how it works. You do not have to be at rock bottom to seek help. It is easier to start earlier.

## Signs it may be time

- Something has been weighing on you for weeks or months and does not fade.
- It gets in the way of work, study, sleep, relationships.
- You start avoiding things that used to matter to you.
- You turn more and more to alcohol, food, screens or something else so you do not have to feel.
- People close to you have noticed a change and are worried.
- You simply feel alone with something and want to talk to someone who understands.

## What if I am not sure?

Talking is a good idea. One consultation does not commit you to anything. It may turn out that the need is smaller than your fear, or the opposite. Start with your family doctor, psychologist or psychotherapist, who can help you work out what fits.

## When it is urgent

If you have thoughts of suicide or self-harm, if you are in immediate danger or worry that someone else is, do not wait. Call **112**. If it is a child, you can also call **116 111**.
