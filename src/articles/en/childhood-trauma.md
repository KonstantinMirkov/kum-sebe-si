Childhood is when we learn what safety is, what trust is and what we can expect from people. When children have instead lived through abuse, neglect, constant fights, fear of a parent, the loss of someone close or an unpredictable environment, their mind adapts. That adaptation is clever for a child, but later it can create difficulties.

## What people often notice as adults

Difficulty trusting. A strong need to please, or the opposite, a need for total control. A sharp sense of guilt or not being enough. Anxiety that seems to have no cause. Trouble with sleep, boundaries, choosing people to be close to. Some do not remember much of their childhood. Others remember everything but it “does not match” their feelings.

This is not a flaw of character. Quite likely it was how you got through.

## It is not your fault

A child cannot be responsible for what the adults around them did or did not do. If part of you still thinks it is, that too is part of the aftermath.

## What can help

Safe, steady relationships in your life today. Therapy with a therapist who has experience with trauma and who does not rush to “dig everything up” at once. The pace is set by you.

If you are a child or teenager and feel unsafe, or you know such a child, call the National Telephone Line for Children **116 111**. In immediate danger, call **112**.
